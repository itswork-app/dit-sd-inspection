# Standing Bracket Instrument Specification

Status: Draft — incomplete reference coverage; official source required  
Instrument: Standing Bracket

## 1. Configuration and versioning

- Standing Bracket is a separate instrument from IFP.
- Checklist categories/items, expected values, photo slots, required status, and applicability must be configurable by authorized Super Admin.
- Instrument configuration is versioned.
- Active sessions freeze the instrument version.
- Historical inspections preserve their original configuration snapshot.

## 3. Checklist

The detailed official Standing Bracket checklist is not present in the currently validated reference baseline. No checklist item is invented in this document.

Before the Standing Bracket specification can be approved, obtain and reconcile:
- official checklist categories and exact item wording;
- expected quantities, dimensions, materials, accessories, and tolerances where applicable;
- inspection method and acceptable/non-compliant conditions;
- required/conditional photo slots and evidence exceptions;
- any serial-number relationship between packaging and bracket;
- official report/PDF display requirements.

## 4. Required validation

1. Obtain complete authorized source checklist and version.
2. Obtain complete original screenshots for every checklist and evidence screen.
3. Confirm whether the five known photo labels are complete.
4. Confirm which evidence slots are mandatory, conditional, or optional.
5. Confirm whether checklist items require notes, measurements, or quantity entry.
6. Confirm official PDF/report grouping and finding presentation.

## 5. Submission behavior

The common inspection engine rules apply: serial claim, versioned checklist, exception-based verification, finding note/evidence, configured exceptions, pre-submit validation, immutable revisions, and server-side submission. Instrument-specific criteria must not be inferred from IFP.
